## How a message travels

Follow mira's message to juno: four steps, two devices, and nothing kept in between. [How it works](/docs/how-it-works) has the details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look up">
    Your phone finds their handle and device keys in the public registry.

    `mira → registry: where is @juno?`

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    It opens an encrypted peer-to-peer connection, directly or through a relay.

    `mira ⇄ juno: encrypted channel open`

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Both sides check each other's device key before any message is accepted.

    `juno: mira's device key ✓`

  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver">
    The message lands on their device, and nowhere else.

    `juno's phone: 1 new message`

  </Step>
</Steps>
