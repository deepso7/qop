title: Using the app
description: Create your qop with an invitation code, save your recovery key, and start a chat by handle.
---

qop runs on iOS and Android. There are no store listings yet.

## Create your qop

You need an invitation code to register. Codes are six characters (`XXX-XXX`) and work once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a handle">
    Tap **Create your qop** and pick the handle people will know you by. Use
    lowercase letters, digits, and underscores, starting with a letter or digit,
    up to 32 characters. The app tells you if it's taken. Handles are permanent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register">
    Enter your invitation code and tap **Register**. The app creates your keys
    on the device, signs the registration, and the qop API submits it to the
    registry. Confirmation usually takes a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery key">
    The app shows your recovery key, which starts with `qop1_`. Tap **Export
    recovery key** and save it somewhere safe outside qop, such as a password
    manager. On Android, confirm with **I saved the recovery key**. Then tap
    **Continue**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Anyone with your recovery key controls your account, and qop can't reset or
  replace it. See [Your recovery key](/docs/devices#your-recovery-key).
</Callout>

You can skip the backup with **I'll save it later**. Profile shows the recovery key as **Not backed up** until you export it.

## Start a chat

1. In **Chats**, start a new chat.
2. Enter the other person's handle and tap **Look up**. The app reads their account straight from the registry.
3. Tap **Start chat**.

Messages are delivered only while the other person's device is reachable. If it isn't, the message shows **Not sent · Tap to retry**. If you've [linked the CLI](/docs/cli) and it's running, your phone hands the message to it instead, and it shows **Waiting on your CLI** until the CLI delivers it.

## Your profile

**Profile** shows your handle and peer ID, your recovery key backup status, and your devices. From there you can:

- Export your recovery key again.
- [Link a device](/docs/devices#link-a-device) such as the CLI, or remove one.
- Log out.

## Log out

Logging out deletes your keys, contacts, and messages from this device. It doesn't remove the device's key from the registry.

<Callout type="danger">
  If you log out without your recovery key, you can't get the account back. The
  app warns you when the key hasn't been exported.
</Callout>

## Permissions

qop doesn't ask for your contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone. It uses:

- **Local network** (iOS) and **Wi-Fi state** (Android) for peer-to-peer connections.
- **Clipboard** only when you paste a pairing code or copy your peer ID.
