title: Introduction
description: qop is a minimal, secure messaging app. Messages go straight from your phone to theirs, with no message servers in between.
---

qop is built on [minip2p](https://minip2p.com). It's open source and self-hostable.

## How it works

- **Peer to peer.** Messages travel directly between devices. qop has no message servers.
- **Keys stay on your devices.** Your phone creates its keys and never sends the private halves to us.
- **Identity on chain.** A public registry maps your handle to your owner address and device keys, so peers can check who they're talking to.
- **No phone number.** You pick a handle instead.

## Use it from the terminal

The qop CLI links to your account as a second device, approved from your phone, and then chats as you. See the [CLI guide](https://github.com/deepso7/qop/tree/main/cli) to set it up.
